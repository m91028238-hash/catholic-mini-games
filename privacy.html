Privacy Policy

The One True Church: Catholic (the app and the website theonetruechurch.grok.me, including its games)
Last updated: 3 November 2026

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information through the app.

Information kept on your device

Some features save your choices, such as saved prayers, game progress and the Desktop/Mobile view setting, on your own device only. This information is not sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Visit counts and hosting

We keep a simple count of visits that does not identify you. Our hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the service running.

Advertising and sharing

There are no adverts, no advertising IDs and no tracking for marketing. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If we add new features, such as a shop or donations, we will update this policy before they go live.

Contact

For any privacy question, email onetruechurch+privacy@mail.grokbot.com.
